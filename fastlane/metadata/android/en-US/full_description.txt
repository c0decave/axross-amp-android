<p>axross-amp plays the music on your phone and shows it: 69 visualizations ("faces") - spectra, scopes, meters, a waterfall, a synthwave sun, a galaxy, a city at night and many more - moving with the music, full screen.</p>

<p><b>Photosensitivity warning:</b> the visualizations move with the music. They are guarded against flashes, red flashes and patterns (WCAG 2.3.1 / 2.3.2), but they are not guaranteed safe for everyone. If you or anyone watching has epilepsy or is sensitive to flashing light, do not use them.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Music from a folder (with its subfolders) or from files, picked with Android's own pickers - no storage permission.</li>
<li>Plays on in the background, with the screen off and on the lock screen; a media notification and lock-screen controls.</li>
<li>Play / pause, previous / next, 15 s back / on, a seek bar - in the app, the notification, the lock screen and from headsets.</li>
<li>The list, the track and the position come back at the next start.</li>
<li>MP3, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, AAC / ALAC in MP4, WAV, AIFF, CAF, MKA; gapless.</li>
<li>Swipe for the next face, tap for the controls, or pick a face from the list.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<p>No network permission and no network code: no tracking, no ads, no update check, no crash reporting, nothing downloaded. It works the same in airplane mode. What it keeps (the list picked last and where it was) stays on the phone.</p>

<p>The player is axross-amp's own library (Rust: symphonia, cpal, egui, wgpu), the same code as on the desktop. Android 9 or later, arm64.</p>
