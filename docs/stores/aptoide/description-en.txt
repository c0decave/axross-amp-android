axross-amp plays the music on your phone and shows it: 69 visualizations ("faces") - spectra, scopes, meters, a waterfall, a synthwave sun, a galaxy, a city at night and many more - moving with the music, full screen.

Photosensitivity warning: the visualizations move with the music. They are guarded against flashes, red flashes and patterns (WCAG 2.3.1 / 2.3.2), but they are not guaranteed safe for everyone. If you or anyone watching has epilepsy or is sensitive to flashing light, do not use them.

Features

- Music from a folder (with its subfolders) or from files, picked with Android's own pickers - no storage permission.
- Plays on in the background, with the screen off and on the lock screen; a media notification and lock-screen controls.
- Play / pause, previous / next, 15 s back / on, a seek bar - in the app, the notification, the lock screen and from headsets.
- The list, the track and the position come back at the next start.
- MP3, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, AAC / ALAC in MP4, WAV, AIFF, CAF, MKA; gapless.
- Swipe for the next face, tap for the controls, or pick a face from the list.

Privacy
No network permission and no network code: no tracking, no ads, no update check, no crash reporting, nothing downloaded. It works the same in airplane mode. What it keeps (the list picked last and where it was) stays on the phone.

The player is axross-amp's own library (Rust: symphonia, cpal, egui, wgpu), the same code as on the desktop. Android 9 or later, arm64.
